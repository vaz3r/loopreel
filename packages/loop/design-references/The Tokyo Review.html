Essential Forms

I.
Ma (間)

The concept of negative space, or interval, as an active element in design, defining relationships and creating presence through absence.

II.
Wabi-Sabi (侘寂)

Embracing imperfection, transience, and the beauty of natural processes, influencing material choices and design longevity.

III.
Shokunin (職人)

The artisan's spirit: dedication to craftsmanship, mastery of materials, and the pursuit of perfection in every detail.

Design Pillars

[02]

Evolution of Form

1950s

Post-War Rebuilding

Focus on functional, modular housing and efficient urban planning, laying groundwork for future growth.

1980s

Metropolitan Expansion

Rapid urbanization, introduction of high-rise architecture, and the emergence of distinct urban districts.

2010s-Present

Sustainable Integration

Emphasis on green building, smart city initiatives, and rethinking human-centric spatial design.
